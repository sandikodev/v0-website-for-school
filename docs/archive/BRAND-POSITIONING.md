# Brand Positioning - AkseSekolah.id

## 🎯 Core Brand Concept

**"Akses Sekolah Digital"**

Bukan sekedar website builder, tapi **platform akses digital lengkap** untuk institusi pendidikan.

---

## 📊 Brand Model: Medium + Lynk.id for Education

### The Pattern

```
Medium.com     → username.medium.com     (Self-branding)
Lynk.id        → username.lynk.id        (Selling)
AkseSekolah.id → schoolname.aksesekolah.id (Education Access)
```

### The Positioning

| Platform | For | Value | Pattern |
|----------|-----|-------|---------|
| **Medium** | Individuals | Publish & build brand | Personal blogging |
| **Lynk.id** | Sellers | Sell & reach customers | E-commerce |
| **AkseSekolah.id** | Institutions | Digital access | Education portal |

```
Awan Kinton = PaaS Provider
PT Koneksi Jaringan Indonesia (Koneksi Cloud)

Partnership Benefits:

✅ Competitive pricing
✅ Enterprise support
✅ Managed services
```

**Value for Schools**:
- Hosting & domain lebih murah
- Setup lebih mudah (terintegrasi)
- Support lokal (Bahasa Indonesia)
- Data di Indonesia (compliance)

### WordPress Ecosystem

**Kami menghargai WordPress sebagai CMS terbaik**

```
WordPress = World's Best CMS
- Largest community
- Highly customizable

- Full data ownership

AkseSekolah.id Position:
"Integrate, not replace"
```

**Integration Model**:
- WordPress as Headless CMS
- AkseSekolah.id as Frontend + SPMB
- Best of both worlds
- School keeps control

**Message**:
```
"Sudah pakai WordPress? Bagus!
 Integrasikan dengan AkseSekolah.id untuk fitur modern.
 WordPress tetap jadi CMS Anda, kami handle sisanya."
```

## ✅ Brand Guidelines Summary

### Do's
✅ Focus on "akses digital" concept
✅ Emphasize ease & completeness
✅ Show real Indonesian schools
✅ Use clear, simple language
✅ Highlight both admin & academic access
✅ Build trust through transparency

### Don'ts
❌ Don't just say "website builder"
❌ Don't use overly technical jargon
❌ Don't compare to unrelated products
❌ Don't hide platform branding
❌ Don't promise what we can't deliver
❌ Don't ignore education context

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

**AkseSekolah.id is not just a website platform.**

It's a **digital access platform** for educational institutions, inspired by the success patterns of Medium (self-branding) and Lynk.id (selling), but focused on a unique niche: **education**.

**Brand Essence**: Digital Access for Education
**Brand Promise**: Easy, Complete, Trusted
**Brand Vision**: Digitalize education in Indonesia and beyond
