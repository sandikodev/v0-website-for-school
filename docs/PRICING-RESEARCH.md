## 🎯 Pricing Rationale

### Why Rp 150k/month (Pro)?

```
Comparison:
- IDWebhost: ~Rp 100k/month (hosting only)
- AkseSekolah.id: Rp 150k/month (hosting + SPMB + features)

Value:
- 50% more expensive than hosting
- But 10x more features
- Zero maintenance
- Professional support

Psychology:
- Rp 150k = affordable for schools
- Not too cheap (perceived quality)
- Not too expensive (budget-friendly)
- Annual discount incentivizes commitment
```

### Why Free Tier?

```
Goals:
1. Viral growth (word of mouth)
2. Brand awareness
3. Market education
4. Lower barrier to entry
5. Upsell opportunity

Limits:
- 100 SPMB submissions/year (enough for small schools)
- 1 admin user (forces upgrade for teams)
- Platform branding (marketing for us)
```

### Why Enterprise at Rp 400k?

```
Target:
- Large schools (>1000 students)
- School networks (multiple schools)
- International schools
- Universities

Value:
- WordPress integration (flexibility)
- Managed hosting (convenience)
- White-label (branding)
- API access (integrations)
- Dedicated support (peace of mind)

Margin:
- Higher price = better support
- Lower volume = manageable
- Premium positioning
```
