## 🚧 Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Slow Adoption
**Mitigation**:
- Free tier for first 50 schools
- Referral program
- Partnership with education associations

### Risk 2: Technical Issues
**Mitigation**:
- Thorough testing
- Monitoring & alerts
- Quick response team

### Risk 3: Competition
**Mitigation**:
- First-mover advantage
- Platform brand
- Network effects
